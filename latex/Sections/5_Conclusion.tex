\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented G2G, the first system that computes base-pair-resolution graph-to-graph alignments of pangenome variation graphs. The reference-anchored diff, the primary validated result, is single-threaded and exact under identical variant representation. It scales near-linearly to an 8-Gbp pair on one core at 100\% precision/recall on planted variants. A parallel snarl-pairing aligner is designed for the general case with the full systems specification: minimiser index, LAPJV matching, priority-dispatch scheduler and vEB layout. This is validated on small-scale depth-1 workloads ($50.1\times$ at 64 threads, $12.7\times$ vs.\ linearise-and-map at $h{=}200$). A biological run confirms the pipeline produces a delta on real data. This shows that graph-to-graph alignment is a viable pangenome-era primitive: the reference-anchored diff demonstrates it concretely, the parallel engine takes a first step toward the general case.

\paragraph{Availability.}
The core engine, together with the synthetic graph generators, plotting scripts, and experiment harnesses used here will be open-sourced after acceptance.
